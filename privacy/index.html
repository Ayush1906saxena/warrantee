Privacy

What we keep, and why.

Your account, your payments, the waitlist and this site, in plain words. How we handle your code is on the security page. Last updated 30 October 2026.
  1. 1

    Who we are

    Warrantee is run by Ayush Saxena, in Bengaluru, India. Ayush Saxena decides how your data is used: the “controller” under European and UK law, and the “data fiduciary” under India’s. For anything about your data, including a complaint, write to our contact page. A person answers.

  2. 2

    What we collect

    For the waitlist: your email address; the optional answers you tap (who you are, your app’s stage, what built it, your country); a WhatsApp number only if you choose to give one; and which form you used. For an account: your name and email address, from Google, GitHub or the email you sign in with. For a payment: the amount, the job it was for, and the payment provider’s reference for it. If you agree in the cookie choices: the pages you read, your browser and device, your approximate location from your IP address, and which ad brought you here. Never your card details.

  3. 3

  5. 5

    Who sees it

    Us, and the services below that do the work for us, each seeing only what its job needs. We don’t sell your data, and we don’t share it for advertising unless you agree to ads cookies. Payments go to Razorpay (India) or Stripe (elsewhere) on their own pages, and they keep your card or bank details under their own policies.

  6. 6

    Data sent abroad

    Some of these services are in the United States or elsewhere outside your country. Where the law asks for it, the transfer is covered by the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework, as each provider offers.

  7. 7

    Your rights

    You can ask us to: See the data we hold about you; Correct it; Delete it; Take a copy of it in a common format; Object to a use of it, or ask us to limit it; and Withdraw consent you gave, as easily as you gave it. Write to our contact page. We answer within 30 days, free of charge, after checking it is really you. If you are not happy with our answer, you can complain to your local data protection authority in the EU, the Information Commissioner’s Office in the UK, or the Data Protection Board of India. In California you also have the right to know, delete and correct, and to opt out of any sale or sharing; we don’t sell, and using a right never changes the service or price you get.

  8. 8

    Children

    Warrantee is for people 18 and over. We don’t knowingly collect data from anyone under 18; if we learn we have, we delete it.

  9. 9

    Keeping it safe

    Everything travels encrypted, access is limited to the people and services that need it, and secrets never reach an AI model. The details are on the security page. If a breach puts your data at risk, we tell you, and the regulator within 72 hours where the law requires it.

  10. 10

    Changes to this notice

    When it changes, this page and its date change first. If a change matters to how we use your data, we email account holders before it applies.

Services that handle data for us

Each one sees only what it needs for the job in its row, under its own privacy policy. If we add or change one, this list changes first.

  • Clerk

    Sign-in, and the email address we write to you at. Sees: Your name, email address and profile picture.

  • Razorpay

    Payments in rupees. Sees: Your payment details, on Razorpay's own checkout. We see the amount and Razorpay's reference.

  • Stripe

    Payments in every other currency. Sees: Your payment details, on Stripe's own checkout. We see the amount and Stripe's reference.

  • Ollama

    The AI models that read and write code for your jobs. Sees: Your job's description and the code it works on. May be processed in the United States.

  • GitHub

    Our GitHub App reads your repository and delivers work as a pull request. GitHub also hosts this site. Sees: The repositories you select for our App. For this site, your visit, as any web host sees it.

  • Cloudflare

    Carries every request to our servers, and forwards email sent to us. Sees: Your IP address and what you send us, in transit. Emails you write to us.

  • Resend

    Sends our emails to you: a job needs you, or is done. Sees: Your email address and the email's text.

  • Hetzner

    The server our service and its database run on. Sees: Everything we keep, as described above.

  • jsDelivr

    Serves the voice files this site downloads if you turn on talking to it. Sees: Your IP address, as any download does.

  • Hugging Face

    Serves the speech models this site downloads if you turn on talking to it. Sees: Your IP address, as any download does. In the United States.

Our code can also use Anthropic’s Claude models and other hosts of open models. None is switched on; each would be added here before it is.
