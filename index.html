01

We write down what your app must always do.

Our planning agent reads your app and drafts its contract in plain English. You change it until it's right, then approve it. Each line becomes an automatic check.

  • Drafted from your code or your idea
  • Sentences you can read, not code
  • Nothing goes on it that can't be checked

Uptime and speed

Your live app is probed around the clock. A slowdown or an outage opens an incident before your customers write in.

Security advisories

Every library your app uses is checked against new advisories. A patch is prepared and proven against your contract.

Incidents, investigated

Operations agents trace an alert to its cause and propose a fix, within strict limits and with every action logged.

Anyone's changes, checked

Hire a freelancer or use an AI builder again: their pull requests are checked against your contract too.

Your app stays where it lives.

We don't move your app onto our servers. We work through the accounts you already have, with access you can see and take back.

Your accounts stay yours

Your code stays on your GitHub, your data in your Supabase, your site on your Vercel or Netlify. You connect them to us with their own sign-in, and can disconnect any time.

Every change, previewed first

Each change gets its own preview of your site and a copy of your database. Your contract runs against the preview before a single user sees it.

A way back, always

If a release misbehaves, your site goes back to its last good version and your database has restore points. Recovery is part of the job, not an afterthought.

Beyond your tests: what nobody told your AI builder.

An app can pass every test and still leak data or fall over. Every app we adopt is checked against this baseline. Each gap comes with proof, gets fixed, and is added to your contract so it stays fixed.

Security

  • Every table only shows each user their own rows
  • Secret keys kept out of the browser and out of your code's history
  • Security headers and cross-site rules set right
  • Libraries with known flaws found and patched

Reliability

  • Calls to outside services that give up and retry instead of hanging
  • Backups switched on, and a restore actually tried
  • Errors reported somewhere a person will see them
  • Lists and queries that stay fast as your data grows

Good practice

  • Test and live keys kept apart
  • A health check your hosting can watch
  • Logs that explain what went wrong
  • Updates kept current, one proven change at a time

Every claim on this site has a date beside it, and the misses are published too. Ask me anything about how it works.

Is this insurance?

No. It's a promise about our own work. If something on your approved contract breaks after a change we made, we fix it at our cost. If we can't fix it, we refund what you paid for that work. For apps we keep, a covered failure that reaches your users also earns credit off next month's fee, up to the whole fee. The terms are a draft until the private beta.

Who does the work?

AI agents: a planner, developers, a test engineer, a reviewer from a different AI company, a judge, and operations agents for live apps. Nothing ships unless it passes the contract you approved, and you merge every change yourself.

What is the contract?

Plain-English sentences about what your app must always do, like "checkout always charges the amount in the cart". Each one becomes an automatic check. You read and approve it before we start, and you can change it any time.

My app was built with an AI builder. Is it for me?

Yes. That is who we built Warrantee for, whichever tool built your app, or if a developer wrote it by hand. If its code can live on GitHub, we can adopt it.

How much does it cost?

One fixed price to adopt your app (write its contract and close the holes), then a monthly fee for keeping it. Both are worked out from your app and fixed before any work starts. Approving a job holds its price, and it's charged only when the work is proven and delivered. Beta members get a free contract preview first.

Do you host my app?

No. Your app keeps running where it runs today, on your own accounts: GitHub, Supabase, Vercel, Netlify. You connect them to us with their own sign-in and can disconnect any time. Outages of those services are covered by their own guarantees, not ours.

Will you touch my live app?

Never without your approval. Every change is tried on a preview of your site and a copy of your database first, and you merge every change yourself. If a release misbehaves, we roll it back.

My app passes its tests. What else do you check?

Security, reliability and good practice: that each user only sees their own data, that secret keys aren't in the browser, that backups are on, that calls to outside services can't hang your app, and more. Every gap comes with proof, gets fixed, and joins your contract.

Can you build a new app too?

Yes. We build it, then keep it working under the same kind of contract.

When can I start?

The private beta opens in January 2027. We'll let people in by fit and by how many apps we can look after safely, starting with apps that are already live.
